meet Daniel

Founder & Head Trainer

Finding Presence in Performance

“Effective communication is not an innate talent but a skill that one develops through dedicated practice.”

Leveraging his background as a performer, Daniel Reece partners with C-suite executives worldwide to refine their communication skills, tailoring the work to each client’s specific industry and needs.

Working with professionals from some of the most successful organizations in politics, finance, sales, legal, tech, healthcare, and more, Daniel’s approach integrates performing arts fundamentals with cognitive analysis rooted in neuroscience to equip clients with the techniques and structure to communicate with confidence.

With over 15 years of coaching and teaching experience, Daniel blends behavioral insights and leadership training to develop impactful communication strategies for his clients. His expertise spans areas such as presentation performance, executive presence, managing speaking anxiety, storytelling, and vocal enhancement. Whether helping professionals prepare for high-stakes presentations or navigate challenging conversations, Daniel’s methods create an authentic and dynamic delivery style tailored to each individual.

Daniel has worked with globally recognized organizations like Google, Netflix, Goldman Sachs, and AstraZeneca, and he holds an MFA from Yale University. In addition to his coaching, Daniel continues to perform on both stage and screen, bringing a unique performer’s perspective to his clients’ growth.
